title: Vehicle-To-Grid Explainer
type: explainer
top_tag: Two-way Charging
description: >-
  Vehicle-To-Grid is when a car charger can flow in both directions, and also be directly
  connected to the electricity network. Connecting to an electricity network requires a
  combination of safety, communication, and capablities that for example Vehicle-To-Load does
  not, and as such this is in general the most complicated of the options. It is also often
  used as a blanket term to refer to two-way charging.
