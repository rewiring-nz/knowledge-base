---
title: Vehicle-To-Load Explainer
type: explainer
top_tag: Two-way Charging
description: >-
  Vehicle-To-Load refers to a car directly powering a device. This could be from a power socket
  inside the car, or a charging plug adapter that then has a socket on the other end. Because
  this is not connected to the grid, there is less complexity and compliance required for it.
  Vehicle-To-Load is generally lower in power than Vehicle-To-Grid. There are a large amount of
  cars in New Zealand which are already available with V2L: EVDB V2L List for NZ
